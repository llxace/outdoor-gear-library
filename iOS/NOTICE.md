## Trail route catalog

[`Resources/DomesticRoutes.json`](Resources/DomesticRoutes.json) contains route data attributed in its metadata to OpenStreetMap contributors and Waymarked Trails and identifies its database license as ODbL-1.0. Preserve that attribution and the license notice when redistributing or adapting the catalog. See [Open Data Commons ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/).

The application source license and the route database license cover different materials. The route catalog is not user inventory data.

## Other services

The app can contact OpenStreetMap/Waymarked Trails for routes, Open-Meteo for weather and geocoding, and a currency-rate service for exchange rates. Apple MapKit supplies map views. Each service and dataset remains subject to its provider's terms and attribution requirements. The app does not bundle user accounts, API keys, or personal inventory records.
